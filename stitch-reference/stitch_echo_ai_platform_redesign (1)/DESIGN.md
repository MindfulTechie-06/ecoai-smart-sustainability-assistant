## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an authoritative, precise, and editorial aesthetic tailored for high-stakes climate intelligence and carbon accounting. Designed for sustainability directors, ESG executives, and enterprise operators, the interface moves past cliché eco-visuals to deliver an institutional, data-dense, and analytically rigorous environment reminiscent of modern financial infrastructure.

The visual direction combines **Contemporary Swiss Modernism** with crisp architectural precision:
- **Atmosphere:** Clean, pristine, grounded, and scientific. Generous negative space balances high-density data matrices, audit logs, and emissions dashboards.
- **Structural Integrity:** Ultra-fine division rules, crisp surface boundaries, and deliberate geometric containment eliminate visual noise.
- **Tone & Demeanor:** Methodical and trustworthy. Color is deployed strictly as signal value—denoting audit readiness, regulatory compliance, and environmental performance vectors without decorative excess.

## Colors

The palette establishes an institutional climate-tech hierarchy anchored in deep mineral greens and organic mineral whites:

- **Deep Jade (`#1A5140`):** The institutional anchor. Applied to high-level structural containers, persistent vertical navigation frames, high-impact numerical headlines, and authoritative structural headers.
- **Emerald Green (`#288760`):** The operational driver. Reserved for primary conversion vectors, key task activations, verified compliance status indicators, and positive decarbonization deltas.
- **Sage Green (`#5CA87C`):** Sub-metric telemetry, secondary chart series, and active contextual sub-states.
- **Soft Mint (`#B7E5BA`):** Tonal badge fills, selection rings, and high-visibility data pill backgrounds when coupled with deep typography.
- **Soft Accent Background (`#DDF3E5`):** Subtle highlight zones, table hover states, and contextual callout panels.
- **Background (`#F7FAF7`) & Card Surfaces (`#FFFFFF`):** A warm, editorial off-white substrate paired with crisp optical white surface tiles to create high legibility without harsh chromatic glare.
- **Borders & Dividers (`#E7EFE9`):** Thin hairline dividers framing modular carbon calculation cards.
- **Text Layers:** Primary typography (`#17251E`) provides uncompromising contrast for micro-labels and financial-grade emissions records, while Secondary typography (`#66756B`) anchors supplementary metadata and units of measurement.

## Typography

The type hierarchy relies entirely on **Manrope**, selected for its geometric rigor, modern proportions, and tabular versatility across numerical reporting.

- **Tabular Numerics:** All data tables, metric tiles, CO2e figures, and time-series ledgers must activate tabular figures (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums; lining-nums;`) to preserve vertical column alignment across thousands of data points.
- **Editorial Micro-Labels:** Small metadata titles, Scope classification headers (Scope 1, Scope 2, Scope 3), and status badges utilize `label-sm` with explicit uppercase transformations and `0.04em` tracking to guarantee readability at scale.
- **Heading Construction:** Headlines feature tight negative letter-spacing to emphasize authority, clean lines, and an editorial presence across high-resolution displays.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout operates on a flexible 12-column grid engine tuned for high-density analysis:

- **Desktop (1280px+):** 12-column layout with a fixed 260px left operational rail (Deep Jade ground), `2rem` gutters, and `3rem` screen canvas margins. Metric card grids consistently span 3 or 4 columns (4-up or 3-up configurations).
- **Tablet (768px - 1279px):** 8-column layout with collapsible sidebar down to an icon-only 64px rail, `1.5rem` gutters, and `2rem` margins. Complex carbon balance sheets default to horizontal pan containers.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column flow with top app header, `1rem` gutters, and `1rem` outer horizontal margins. Full multi-column data cards collapse into stacked modular rows.
- **Rhythm & Whitespace:** Density inside data cells remains tight and structured (`space-xs` to `space-sm`), while negative space between functional modules is expansive (`space-xl` to `space-2xl`), creating clear boundaries without requiring heavy graphic borders.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is achieved through layered tonal planes, hairline delineation, and low-opacity, jade-tinted ambient shadows rather than stark structural drop-shadows.

- **Flat Foundation:** Ground canvas resides at `#F7FAF7`. Workspace components and data tables rise exactly one step up to `#FFFFFF`.
- **Delicate Outlines:** Every card, container, and flyout employs an explicit 1px boundary using `#E7EFE9`.
- **Tonal Tiers:** Nested containers (such as breakdown calculations within an audit report) drop slightly back into `#F7FAF7` or forward into `#DDF3E5` rather than relying on shadow stacking.
- **Ambient Shadow System:**
  - *Base Cards & Metric Tiles:* `0 1px 3px rgba(23, 37, 30, 0.05), 0 10px 25px -5px rgba(26, 81, 64, 0.06)`
  - *Floating Menus & Dropdowns:* `0 4px 12px rgba(23, 37, 30, 0.06), 0 20px 32px -8px rgba(26, 81, 64, 0.10)`
  - *Modal Dialogs & Slide-out Drawers:* `0 12px 36px rgba(23, 37, 30, 0.10), 0 32px 64px -16px rgba(26, 81, 64, 0.16)`

## Shapes

The interface embraces a precise, slightly softened architectural geometry (Level 1: Soft). Corners are disciplined and restrained to preserve the platform's professional, audit-grade credibility.

- **Default UI (`0.25rem` / 4px):** Form inputs, checkboxes, small segmented controls, and metric status tags.
- **Container Surfaces (`0.5rem` / 8px):** Metric scorecards, analytical graph frames, dialogs, and primary structural cards.
- **Pills (`9999px`):** Reserved exclusively for status indicators, Scope attribution badges (e.g., `SCOPE 1`), and contextual state indicators.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Background in Emerald Green (`#288760`), label in crisp `#FFFFFF`, 8px border radius, with a subtle single-pixel border (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.15)` inset). Hover transition shifts to `#1A5140`.
- **Secondary:** Surface `#FFFFFF`, border 1px solid `#E7EFE9`, text `#17251E`. Hover triggers a background transition to `#F7FAF7` with an active border tint of `#5CA87C`.
- **Ghost:** Transparent background with `#1A5140` typography, shifting to `#DDF3E5` on interaction.

### Chips & Badges
- **Status Pills:** Background `#DDF3E5`, text `#1A5140`, 1px border `#B7E5BA`.
- **High-Alert / Non-Compliant:** Background `#FEECEC`, text `#8A1E1E`, border `#F8C8C8`.
- **Scope Categorization:** `label-sm` uppercase type, `0.25rem` horizontal padding, paired with a solid 6px indicator dot representing fuel, grid, or supply-chain vectors.

### Cards & Metrics
- Pure `#FFFFFF` surface framed with 1px `#E7EFE9`.
- Header areas feature secondary metadata in `#66756B` alongside deep `#1A5140` title text. Primary measurements are rendered via `tabular-stat-lg` in `#17251E`, with unit descriptors (e.g., `tCO2e`) displayed in regular body weighting.

### Form Inputs & Selectors
- Background `#FFFFFF`, 1px border `#E7EFE9`, 4px radius. 
- Focus state replaces the border with `#288760` alongside an external 3px halo in `rgba(40, 135, 96, 0.15)`. Typography defaults to `#17251E` with placeholder values in `#66756B`.

### Lists & Data Tables
- Header rows utilize `#F7FAF7` with 1px border-bottom in `#E7EFE9`, displaying `label-sm` uppercase type in `#66756B`.
- Content rows sit on `#FFFFFF`, transitioning to `#DDF3E5` at 40% opacity on cursor hover. Border dividers between cells are strictly 1px `#E7EFE9`.

### Specialized Climate Indicators
- **Intensity Bars:** Slim 4px track rails set to `#E7EFE9`, filled with `#288760` (or dynamic gradient to `#5CA87C`) denoting progress toward Net Zero targets.
- **Audit Verification Stamp:** Subtle icon-and-label pairing featuring `#1A5140` typography nested inside a soft `#B7E5BA` pill outline.
